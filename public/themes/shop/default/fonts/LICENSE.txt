The fonts in this folder are Karla, Mukta, Baloo 2, Archivo, Manrope, Poppins, DM Serif Display and
Noto Sans Arabic, as served by Google Fonts (https://fonts.google.com), subset to the latin,
latin-ext and (Noto Sans Arabic) arabic ranges. Each is copyright its authors, as named on its Google
Fonts page, and licensed under the SIL Open Font License, Version 1.1:
https://openfontlicense.org/open-font-license-official-text/

They are listed, with their weights and subsets, in packages/DigitalLabs/Shop/src/Config/fonts.php.
